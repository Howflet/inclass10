1. Trace the local tip feature from button tap to displayed text. 
Which widget/state changes during the simulated loading state, 
and how does the user recover from the local error case?
Answer: _tipLoading becomes true, _tipText and _tipError become null.
2. Why must the app keep form validation local even when it can ask an AI for a tip? 
Give one example of a bad decision if AI were allowed to approve signup.
Answer: Validation is a fixed rule the app enforces every time and the AI tip would be 
the only advice. An example of a bad decision is an AI looking at at password like "pass" 
and an email like "bad_email" and approving it for signup despite it being bad data.
